The archive is the feature.

If you cannot group side chats yet, make one pinned chat do the job.

Do this today: create a side chat named Summarized Side Chats, pin it at the top, and add every new chat to it the day you create it.

Why this fixes the actual problem

You do not need a perfect taxonomy. You need one reliable place that answers: “What chats do I have, and what is each one for?” Pinning is the grouping until grouping exists.
